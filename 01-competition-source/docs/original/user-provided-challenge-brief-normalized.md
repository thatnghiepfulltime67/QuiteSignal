## The Challenge

We're looking for builders who can take a real, impactful open-source protocol
and add privacy or build/merge a project truly innovative with Nox.

Don't just build a proof-of-concept. We want to see a protocol integration clean
enough that it could become a real product. By integrating Nox into existing
open-source infrastructure, you're enhancing it with privacy at the core.

These protocols aren't built for privacy; they're public by design. Build on top
of them using Nox to maintain privacy while interacting with transparent
infrastructure through batching, layering, or other mechanisms.

Maintain the privacy guarantee without needing to modify the underlying
protocols. Route a swap through Nox without breaking composability—that's where
the real value lies.

Suggested open-source targets (not mandatory):

- Wallets such as MetaMask, Rabby, and Rainbow.
- DeFi such as Aave, Uniswap, and Curve.
- Treasury such as Safe, Sablier, and Superfluid.

Projects are judged on how cleanly Nox integrates into the app, the privacy it
adds, and how close the result is to something a company can deploy.

Any builder reusing a project from the previous Vibe Coding Hackathon will be
disqualified.

## What is Nox

Nox is described as a confidential computing layer that enables computations on
encrypted data while preserving DeFi composability. It combines on-chain smart
contracts with off-chain Trusted Execution Environments to process encrypted
data without exposing plaintext on-chain.

Resources: https://linktr.ee/iexec.tech
